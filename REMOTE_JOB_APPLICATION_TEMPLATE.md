## 7. Interview story (STAR) — use when asked "tell me about a project"

- **Situation:** Five aviation refuellers, two Excel workbooks, everything typed twice. Dates and task names were inconsistent, the availability formula broke every month, and late maintenance carried contract penalties nobody saw coming.
- **Task:** As coordinator, I wanted one place to record work once and get reports and early warnings automatically, without changing what the client receives.
- **Action:** Analysed three years of records, wrote a spec with roles, rules and acceptance tests, built the app with an AI coding agent, cleaned and migrated the history, and trained the technicians on their phones.
- **Result:** In production since October 2026. Each job is recorded once. Availability, PM compliance and deadline alerts are live, and the client's Excel reports are generated in their existing format.

Have the demo open on your phone during interviews and show the role switch and the "cannot close without repair" rule.
