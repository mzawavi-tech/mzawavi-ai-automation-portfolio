### 5. Aviation Bowser Maintenance — PPM, CM & Procurement Readiness

**BSA evidence:** Operational process understanding, workflow design, exception visibility, reminder integration and coordination between operations, maintenance and procurement.

## My Business Systems Analysis approach

1. **Understand the business objective** — who needs the system, what outcome matters and what problem exists today.
2. **Discover the current process (AS-IS)** — actors, triggers, inputs, outputs, hand-offs, pain points and exceptions.
3. **Define the target process (TO-BE)** — workflow stages, business rules, roles and expected outcomes.
4. **Clarify requirements** — functional behaviour, data needs, integrations, validation and acceptance criteria.
5. **Coordinate implementation** — communicate requirements and dependencies with developers, vendors and business stakeholders.
6. **Support testing and UAT** — relate test scenarios to requirements, capture issues and follow up resolutions.
7. **Support migration and rollout** — coordinate readiness, user communication, operational handover and follow-up.
8. **Improve from feedback** — review actual usage, exceptions and stakeholder feedback.

This describes my working approach and transferable capability. It is not a claim that I have delivered every listed artefact on a card-management implementation.
